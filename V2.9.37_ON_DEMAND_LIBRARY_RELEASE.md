# SM EDUCATION v2.9.37 — On-Demand Textbook Library

Base: v2.9.36 Textbook Knowledge, originally based on v2.9.35.

## Architecture decision
The app package contains no textbook PDF collection. It stays lightweight. The national library is a catalogue/discovery and local-import experience, not a bulk-download bundle or mandatory central sync.

## User flow
1. Choose the school level and subject.
2. Open the official ONPS portal to locate the desired title, or choose a legally obtained PDF.
3. Import only that selected PDF.
4. The selected book is saved locally in IndexedDB, then indexed page-by-page for in-app reading and teacher retrieval.
5. Delete an imported book at any time to reclaim device storage. Other titles are never downloaded automatically.

## Included capabilities
- Internal PDF reader and in-app download for imported books.
- Page-level local text search with book title, subject, grade, and page number.
- Consent-gated excerpts to the smart teacher.
- No preloaded textbook PDFs, no silent bulk downloads, and no mandatory cloud storage.

## Limits
- ONPS is linked as the official discovery portal; the app does not invent direct PDF links.
- Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR to become searchable.
- Local books/indexes remain on the device/browser profile unless a future optional sync feature is explicitly enabled.
