- Matching uses grade + subject + term + topic + lesson (not title alone), preserving repeated lesson titles across terms/topics. All 1,581 exercise records remain linked.
- Encyclopedia records without a matching deep lesson remain independently searchable.
- AI grounding includes encyclopedia learning targets/key ideas/procedures and an explicit warning that platform-derived content is not an official ministry document.
- Local lesson and exercise answers disclose their platform-derived/practice source status.
- Smart Teacher status reports encyclopedia record counts.
- Service-worker cache revision advanced to `sm-school-ai-v2.9.40-shell-r2-b12`.
