# SM éducation v2.9.41 — Smart Teacher & Smart Generator

## Release objective
This release focuses on the two competitive AI workflows: the Smart Teacher and the Smart Generator. It is a semantic-version update from 2.9.40, not merely a cache revision.

## Smart Teacher
- First-pass retrieval now receives the request's explicit grade and subject, preventing same-topic matches from unrelated grades/subjects from dominating the local answer or inferred subject.
- The local fallback uses the same requested grade scope.
- Retrieved platform records are labelled KB-1…KB-3 with their record IDs and an explicit note that no verified page number is provided.
- Gemini instructions require citations only to supplied KB labels and prohibit invented IDs, page numbers, official approvals, or official curriculum sequence claims.

## Smart Generator
- AI-generated exercise sets must contain exactly the requested number of questions.
- Each accepted question must include a model answer and at least one non-empty solution step; duplicate question prompts reject the set.
- Generated marks are allocated against the requested total after structural validation.
- Claude draft validation rejects duplicate questions, overly long question/answer text, and solutions with fewer than two steps. Teacher review remains mandatory and publication to the exercise bank remains disabled.
- Claude-generated choices are preserved in the preview data.
