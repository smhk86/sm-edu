# SM éducation v2.9.42 — AI Quality & Evidence Guardrails

## Scope
This release continues the v2.9.41 focus on Smart Teacher and Smart Generator. It adds deterministic safeguards around source citations and generated question structure. It does not claim to rank Gemini against Claude without paired live outputs and teacher-reviewed reference answers.

## Smart Teacher
- Added `SM_TEACHER_CORE.auditCitations(answer, kbContext)`.
- The tutor fetch wrapper checks `[KB-n]` labels in successful AI answers against the exact retrieved `kb_context`.
- Unknown labels are flagged in `citation_audit` metadata and a visible Arabic warning is appended to the answer.
- This verifies citation-label existence, not the semantic truth of the cited claim; teachers must still review the source content.

## Smart Generator
- Limits exercise groups to 10 and questions to 20.
- Rejects questions longer than 1,600 chars, answers longer than 3,000 chars, explanations longer than 2,000 chars, and solution steps longer than 1,200 chars.
- Requires 2–8 non-empty solution steps.
- When choices are provided, requires 2–6 bounded, non-empty, unique choices.
- Retains exact requested question count, answer, duplicate-prompt and mark-total checks.
