# SM Education v2.9.23 — Formative Feedback

- Correct answers receive positive feedback and the available verified explanation.
- Incorrect answers receive a scaffolded hint without revealing the correct option, allowing the learner to retry.
- The selected answer, reasoning, attempt count and feedback state remain attached to the question while navigating between questions.
